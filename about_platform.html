Student Learning Platform

Why I made this platform

I built this site to give you one simple place to study for my courses. It combines practice quizzes with a growing library of supplemental learning resources so you can choose the kind of support that is most useful to you.

What you can do here

After signing in, choose your course and then decide whether you want to practice with quiz questions or browse the resource library.

Practice Quizzes Build a practice quiz from the topics you want to review and get immediate feedback as you work through the questions.
Resource Library Browse optional flashcards, videos, and other study resources organized by course topic.

How the practice quizzes work

Each quiz gives you a random set of questions from the topics you selected. Behind the scenes, the platform keeps track of which questions you answered correctly and incorrectly. Questions you miss become more likely to show up again, while questions you answer correctly become less likely to repeat. This helps the system naturally focus more on what you still need to learn.

About the resource library

The resource library is meant to make useful study materials easier to find without requiring you to search through several different sites. Resources are organized by course and topic, with separate sections for things such as flashcards and videos.

Everything in the resource library is optional and supplemental. These resources are there to support your learning; they are not required course activities and do not replace the syllabus, course announcements, or materials provided in class.

The library is still under construction. I am gradually importing resources from my previous course websites, and the Resource Library page will continue to link you to those sites while that transition is in progress.

Designed for quick, flexible studying

I wanted the platform to work well on both phones and computers so you can use it whenever you have a few minutes to study. On a computer, the quiz interface also supports keyboard shortcuts to make answering questions faster.

Please let me know about errors

Many of the practice questions were created with AI and then reviewed by me. I do my best to check them carefully, but there is still a chance that a question may contain an error or that you may encounter a technical problem. If that happens, please use the feedback option so I can review it and improve the platform.

You can also report problems with resources, such as broken links or outdated content, as those feedback features are added to the library.

The module-code section is not in regular use yet. I plan to use it for additional guided learning modules in the future.
